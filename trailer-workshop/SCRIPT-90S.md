## Production constraints

- This is a 90-second edit plan, not a measured finished audio track. Record the dialogue at a natural conversational pace first. Trim reaction handles or optional silent inserts if needed; keep both death-card holds and five-second final card.
- Each timestamp covers several short shots, not one long video-generation request. Reuse the apartment, café and void camera setups across lives to reduce new environments. Rook can be phone audio only; the gang needs one entrance shot and one reused reflection/camera insert.
- Generate clean phone plates. Composite setup, backup, receive, shield and unshield labels in the editor. Do not ask a video model to maintain readable wallet text.
- All five requested lesson groups appear: setup; getting ZEC through earned payment; shielding/unshielding; sending; receiving. This is a teaser, not a complete wallet tutorial.
- Shielding does not erase the original public transaction. Unshielding is legitimate but exposes public output information; amount/timing correlation is a clue, not proof. Off-chain cameras supply the gang's physical identification. Shielded transactions do not make Niko physically invisible.
