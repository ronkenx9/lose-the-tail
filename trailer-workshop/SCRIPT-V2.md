# LOSE THE TAIL — “Again.”

Dialogue draft 02 · Natural ensemble performance · Approximately 9–11 minutes pending a scratch read

This replaces the clipped dialogue treatment in SCRIPT.md. It is a longer story-trailer/short-film draft; a short promotional cut can be edited from it later. No voiceover has been generated. Time is a planning range, not an audio measurement.

## Performance direction

People talking to each other, not seven narrators delivering taglines. Let questions change the next person's tone. Allow a laugh to fail, a sentence to be interrupted, a reply to come before someone has finished. Ellipses indicate a genuine thought or hesitation, not a compulsory dramatic pause after every phrase.

Niko is conversational and increasingly vulnerable. Rook is warm and familiar. Auntie Node teases while working. Mika sounds comfortable and practical. Needle reports quickly. The Tailor is courteous and patient in a way that feels invasive. Zero enjoys language and jokes, but becomes direct when Niko is genuinely frightened. His theatricality is an acting reference, not a celebrity voice imitation.

Preserve the original Zilkroad face designs. Express through movement, timing, head angle and voice. Use the new full-body references. No invented human lips, eyebrows or hair. Wallet UI is editorial overlay with SIMULATED ZEC clearly established. Rewinds reset the practice game, not real blockchain transactions.

## 1. The call

Rain taps the apartment window. The phone vibrates against an empty cup. Niko fumbles for it, knocks the cup over, catches it a moment too late.

**NIKO — thick with sleep, trying to sound awake:**
“Hello? Yeah, I'm up. I've been up. What's happening?”

**ROOK — smiling; he knows perfectly well:**
“You've been up? Then why do you sound like you're speaking from under a mattress?”

**NIKO — pulls the blanket off his head:**
“Because the reception's better under here. What's going on?”

**ROOK:**
“I checked the work you sent over. It's good. A little late, but good. I've got five ZEC for you.”

Niko stops rubbing his face.

**NIKO — cautious excitement:**
“Wait. You're paying me now? I thought you said next week.”

**ROOK:**
“And I thought you'd complain less if I paid you early. Apparently I was wrong.”

**NIKO — a real laugh:**
“No, no, absolutely no complaints. Give me a second. I haven't actually set the wallet up yet.”

**ROOK — settles into helping:**
“All right. Open the wallet and create one. Take your time with the backup. Keep your recovery phrase somewhere safe and private. Don't send it to me. I only need the receiving address.”

Niko follows the steps. Recovery details remain concealed. He checks the screen instead of blindly tapping.

**NIKO:**
“Okay. Backup's safe. This is the receiving address… there. Sent it.”

**ROOK:**
“Got it. I'm sending to the transparent address you shared. You'll see the payment when it comes through.”

A short editorial time cut. Payment confirmation: RECEIVED 5 ZEC · TRANSPARENT · PRACTICE WALLET.

**NIKO — surprised by how real it feels:**
“Oh. There it is. It's actually there.”

**ROOK — fondly:**
“Yes, that's usually the best part of getting paid. Go eat something. You sound like you've been surviving on ambition.”

**NIKO:**
“Ambition and half a packet of biscuits. Thank you. Seriously.”

## 2. The restaurant

Warm window, wet pavement, a bell over the door. Auntie Node is wiping a dark ceramic bowl. Niko comes in trying not to look as pleased with himself as he is.

**AUNTIE NODE — without looking up:**
“Ah. You're smiling. Either someone paid you, or you're about to ask me for credit.”

**NIKO:**
“Someone paid me. You could at least pretend to be happy for me.”

**AUNTIE NODE — now looking up, delighted despite herself:**
“I am happy for you. I'm even happier for the bill you owe me. What are you having?”

**NIKO:**
“The usual. Extra noodles. And the good sauce, please. Not the one you give people you don't like.”

**AUNTIE NODE:**
“It's the same sauce. I just give you less of it.”

Niko laughs and scans the payment request. The café uses a transparent receiving address in this first life. He confirms without reading much else.

**NIKO:**
“There you go. Tell me that came through before I start eating like a rich man.”

**AUNTIE NODE — checking:**
“It came through. Eat like someone who remembers he still has rent.”

Cut away from their warmth. A public payment record appears on another screen. Separately, a café camera supplies Niko's image.

## 3. The Tailors

Needle watches from under an awning. Hem waits just outside the restaurant. The Tailor doesn't hurry to answer the radio.

**NEEDLE — low, businesslike:**
“Found the address. Five came in, then a payment to the noodle place. Camera's got him at the counter. Brown face, dark band across the eyes.”

**TAILOR — distracted interest becoming attention:**
“Already spending it? Good for him. Stay where you are. I'd hate to interrupt a celebration too early.”

The bell rings. Niko looks up. Auntie's working rhythm stops. Hem occupies the exit while the Tailor approaches the counter.

**TAILOR — pleasant enough to be mistaken for a customer:**
“Don't get up. You've only just sat down. Five ZEC is a good morning's work. You ought to enjoy it.”

**NIKO — a nervous laugh, looking to Auntie:**
“Sorry, have we met? How do you know what I got paid?”

**TAILOR:**
“We haven't met. That's what I find so interesting. I knew what arrived, where it went next… and then that helpful little camera told us which table to visit.”

**NIKO — the joke has gone out of his voice:**
“Look, I don't want any trouble. I just came in here to get breakfast.”

**AUNTIE NODE — firm, keeping fear out of it:**
“Then let him eat. Whatever this is, take it outside my shop.”

**TAILOR — doesn't look away from Niko:**
“Of course. We wouldn't dream of making a mess.”

Niko shifts toward the other side of the counter. Needle is already there.

**NIKO — breath rising:**
“Hold on. Wait. You don't have to—”

Cut before contact. Black. YOU DIED. The restaurant noise disappears. Hold the silence.

## 4. Someone in the dark

Niko wakes in the void, reaching for the counter that isn't there. Zero is watching him from a slight tilt.

**NIKO — panicked, still searching for the room:**
“Where's the shop? Where did everybody go? I was just—”

**ZERO — measured, mildly apologetic:**
“Yes. You were. And then things took a rather unpleasant turn. Take a moment. You're allowed to be upset.”

**NIKO:**
“Upset? That man knew what I'd been paid. He knew where I was. I didn't tell him anything.”

**ZERO — the humor drains just enough:**
“You didn't tell him in a conversation. You left a public trail. Then a camera helped him put a face to it.”

Niko looks at his phone. It won't wake.

**NIKO — smaller now:**
“Am I actually dead?”

**ZERO:**
“In this life? Yes.”

**NIKO:**
“That's a horrible way to start a sentence. What do you mean, 'in this life'? And who are you?”

**ZERO — a small, almost friendly tilt:**
“You can call me Zero. As for what I am… let's get you through breakfast before we complicate the relationship.”

**NIKO — anger returning because it feels safer:**
“So you watched all that happen?”

**ZERO — lets the accusation land:**
“I did. And I'm offering you another try. The money isn't the mistake. You need to understand what your choices reveal.”

A little white cube forms between them. Inside it, the apartment window fills with rain again.

**ZERO — gentler:**
“When you get paid, shield the funds. I'll explain as we go. You don't have to work it all out while someone's blocking the door.”

**NIKO — looks from the cube to Zero:**
“And I get to remember this?”

**ZERO:**
“I would strongly recommend it.”

## 5. Second life

The phone vibrates in the same place. Niko catches it before it hits the cup. He checks the doorway, then the window.

**ROOK — on the repeating call:**
“You've been up? Then why do you sound like—”

**NIKO — interrupts, rattled:**
“Under a mattress. I know. Sorry. Just… give me a minute.”

Editorial montage resets the practice payment. Niko opens SHIELD, actually reads the screen, and confirms. Transparent funds move into the shielded balance after confirmation.

**NIKO — asking instead of declaring:**
“Okay, I've shielded it. Does that mean they can't see the payment from Rook anymore?”

**ZERO:**
“No. That part was already public. Shielding doesn't reach backward and erase it. It changes what you reveal as you use the funds from here.”

**NIKO:**
“So I haven't disappeared.”

**ZERO — dryly, while a pedestrian nearly bumps into him:**
“You're standing in the middle of the pavement. No, you haven't disappeared.”

Niko moves aside. He laughs once, mostly at himself.

## 6. The shortcut

At an exchange window, Niko selects a transparent recipient. The interface warns that the recipient and output amount will be public. He skims it.

**NIKO — thinking aloud, impatient to feel safe:**
“If I send it out from here, I can get on with my day. It's been shielded already. That should be enough, shouldn't it?”

**ZERO — begins to intervene:**
“Look at the recipient before you—”

Niko confirms. UNSHIELD · PUBLIC RECIPIENT + AMOUNT. A short time cut shows confirmation.

**NIKO:**
“I already did it. What? What have I done?”

The Tailor studies two public boundary amounts and nearby times. A dotted line is labeled POSSIBLE MATCH.

**TAILOR — almost admiring the convenience:**
“Nearly the same amount, and barely any time between them. That doesn't prove it's him. But it does give us somewhere to look. Check the exchange camera.”

**NEEDLE:**
“That's him. At the window.”

Niko sees Hem reflected in the glass.

**NIKO — exhausted disbelief turning into fear:**
“No. No, I did what you said. I shielded it.”

Hem moves. Cut to black. YOU DIED. AGAIN.

## 7. A proper conversation

Niko sits on an invisible curb. Zero settles beside him, leaving a little space. Neither speaks immediately.

**NIKO — tired now, no performance left:**
“I thought I understood it. I pressed the thing. It said shielded. For about five minutes, I thought I could stop worrying.”

**ZERO — entirely sincere:**
“You did shield it. Then you sent to a transparent recipient. That output exposed the recipient and amount. They used the timing to make a guess, and the camera did the rest.”

**NIKO:**
“So unshielding is bad?”

**ZERO:**
“Not automatically. Sometimes it's what you need to do. But you should know what becomes public before you confirm. A warning is only useful if you give yourself time to read it.”

**NIKO — embarrassed, with a little defensive humor:**
“You could have led with that.”

**ZERO — accepts it, then lets the joke back in:**
“I was getting there. You have a remarkably fast thumb.”

Niko almost smiles.

**NIKO:**
“All right. Suppose I want to pay someone without putting the payment details out for everyone to inspect. What am I looking for?”

**ZERO:**
“A shielded payment to someone who can receive it that way. Check the recipient. Check the amount. Read what the wallet says about the transaction. Then send.”

**NIKO:**
“And they still know I've paid them?”

**ZERO:**
“Of course. Privacy from the public doesn't mean keeping the payment secret from the person you're paying.”

Niko holds out his hand for the cube.

**NIKO — steadier:**
“Okay. Again. But this time, let me do it slowly.”

## 8. Practicing with a friend

Third life. Niko is already sitting up when the phone vibrates. Later, Mika waits by an arcade counter. He shows her the payment request without exposing any recovery information.

**NIKO:**
“Can I test something with you? I'm going to send a little ZEC. Just tell me when it arrives.”

**MIKA — amused by his intensity:**
“Sure. You look like you're about to defuse something. What's going on?”

**NIKO:**
“I've had a very long morning.”

He checks that the route is shielded, reviews the recipient and amount, and confirms. Editorial time passes before her confirmation.

**MIKA — looking down, then back at him:**
“There it is. Yeah, I've got it. Want me to send it back?”

**NIKO:**
“Please. Use this receiving request.”

She reviews and sends. His phone later shows SHIELDED PAYMENT RECEIVED.

**NIKO — shoulders finally release:**
“Oh. Right. So I can see it, you can see your part… but the whole city doesn't get the details.”

**MIKA:**
“Exactly. Now, are we getting food, or are you going to keep staring at that?”

**NIKO — laughing properly:**
“Food. Definitely food.”

## 9. The same bowl

The same bell. The same counter. Auntie is wiping the same bowl. Niko pauses at the door, then steps inside.

**AUNTIE NODE:**
“Ah. You're smiling. Either someone paid you—”

**NIKO — quietly enjoying the familiarity:**
“Someone paid me. And yes, I remember the bill.”

**AUNTIE NODE — approving:**
“Then we're going to get along beautifully. The usual?”

**NIKO:**
“Please. And can I pay you shielded?”

**AUNTIE NODE — matter-of-fact, showing her request:**
“You can. Here you go. Check it before you send.”

He does. This time, we hold long enough to see him read. Confirmation. She slides over the bowl.

**AUNTIE NODE:**
“All sorted. Eat it while it's hot.”

Across town, the Tailor waits for a public payment clue that doesn't appear. Old evidence remains on his screen; nothing is magically erased.

**NEEDLE — on the radio:**
“No new payment details to work with. What do you want us to do?”

The Tailor turns his phone face down.

**TAILOR — softly irritated:**
“For the moment? Stop telling me how little we know.”

Back at the counter. Niko takes a bite. A long, ordinary breath out.

**ZERO — close, warm, without a grand echo:**
“There you are. You can actually taste it this time.”

**NIKO — mouthful implied through timing, not facial redesign:**
“I was starting to think breakfast was too ambitious.”

**ZERO — a quiet laugh:**
“Only when you insist on skipping the instructions.”

Niko watches the rain thin outside. He stays to finish his food.

## 10. End card

Dawn over Ledger City. The music opens up without drowning the street sounds.

**ZERO — a gentle invitation, not a command:**
“You don't have to learn everything in one morning. Start with your wallet. Understand what you're sharing. Then take the next step.”

LOSE THE TAIL
Learn Zcash. One life at a time.
Play the practice game: lose-the-tail.vercel.app
Simulated funds. Real privacy lessons.

Hold the end card for at least five seconds. Last sound: the café bell.

## Accuracy and edit notes

- Getting ZEC is shown as an earned payment from Rook; the wallet does not create free money.
- Setup, secure backup, transparent receiving, shielding, unshielding, shielded sending and shielded receiving all have visible actions and conversational explanations.
- Public transactions do not inherently reveal a street address or a person's face. Fictional off-chain cameras provide that link in the story.
- Shielding does not erase public history. Similar boundary amounts and timing support inference, not guaranteed linkage. Unshielding is not inherently wrong, and there is no guaranteed anonymity timer.
- Shielded payment participants know their own transaction information; the public does not receive the same payment details. Privacy does not make someone physically invisible or eliminate every form of surveillance.
- Confirmation scenes use editorial time cuts. No promise of instantaneous settlement.
- Record a natural scratch read before locking runtime. If a shorter trailer is needed, remove whole exchanges or scenes; do not remove every connective word or speed the actors into unnatural speech.
- Technical grounding carried forward from the reviewed first draft: https://z.cash/learn/how-to-use-zcash/ and https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/master/rtd_pages/privacy_recommendations_best_practices.html
