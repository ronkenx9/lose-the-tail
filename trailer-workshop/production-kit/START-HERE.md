# START HERE — voxel-noir production kit / V2

Use VIDEO-PROMPTS-VISIBLE-SCREENS.md for the new ready-to-reference version. It supersedes the old blanket “blank screen” instruction. Keep the 90-second schedule.

## What to upload
For each shot, upload only its named character sheets, original NFT faces, and the screen or monitor images listed below. Continuity boards are written layout instructions, not scenery to place in the film. If your tool accepts few images, prioritize the character face and the current screen state. Do not upload this whole pack in one generation.

## Phone and monitor shots
The PNGs are full screen CONTENT, not pictures of a phone: map their edges to the physical display. Preserve the matte-black handset and right-hand grip. For a sequence with several states, use the numbered PNGs in order, one short take per state if necessary, then cut at the tap. Never tile all screen states into one phone. No face or mouth animation is added.

Use this instruction with a screen reference: “Display the supplied screen image inside the physical phone display. Preserve its composition, amounts and labels. Keep the entire display visible and steady. Do not leave it blank; do not place the screen image as a floating panel. The rest of the shot follows the character and camera prompt.”

A video model may distort exact text. The same supplied PNG can replace the screen in an editor, using its four corners and masking fingers in front. This is a fallback using included materials, not another asset you need to find. Avoid stretching a phone screen to a different aspect ratio; fit it proportionally within the glass.

The official Zcash icon and wordmark are in branding/. Upload them only when adding the Zcash mark. The supplied wallet is a fictional game practice wallet with its own title; it is not an official Zcash app. The PNG screens are complete without adding a logo.

## Ready-made graphics
Use the death cards directly for one second each. Use the end card directly for five seconds. The transparent game title can fade over S25. Do not spend video-generation credits recreating these cards. Both death cards remain silent.

## Voices and sound
The audio/ folder has the 17 current v4 clips and the V3 90-second preview. Needle is V05; Zero uses Brian. The audio manifest provides measured placement times and supersedes estimated cue times in older documents. sfx/ contains seven original synthesized effects/ambience beds, optional for editing; they are not recorded location sound or a finished musical score. Keep dialogue above effects. No additional voice generation is required.

## Environment continuity
The included continuity boards lock layout, light, and props. Generate the first environment shot with its board, then reuse an approved frame from that shot as an optional additional reference on returns. These frames come from your video generation, so they cannot be supplied as actual footage in advance. There are no missing external stock images to download. The old requirement for a café-camera still on the monitor is replaced by a self-contained designed camera-observation panel. No required camera inset remains empty.
