# LOSE THE TAIL — “Again.”

Workshop draft 01 · Target 3:20 · Cinematic voxel story trailer · Original dialogue

Creative choice: three lives, two deaths, one ordinary breakfast finally earned.
This draft follows the existing game's characters and onboarding premise. Niko is a new name for the lead; Rook is promoted from client to working mentor for this trailer. These are proposed story choices, not changes to the game. No voiceover or video has been generated.

## Performance reference before recording

Use the Avengers films as a performance study, not a sound-alike target:
- **Ultron in Avengers: Age of Ultron:** take the conversational theatricality, the amused pause before a cutting observation, and the change from almost-friendly to unsettling. Give that rhythm to Zero, with compassion underneath. Let a joke land without a musical punchline.
- **Thanos in Avengers: Infinity War:** take the stillness, patient certainty, and the sense that he can wait longer than anyone else. Give that weight to the Tailor. He threatens with precision, never a generic monster roar.
- Original actors or distinct synthetic human voices for every role. No copied Marvel lines, character names in the final voice prompts, or imitation of a specific actor's vocal identity. Do not process everyone into the same trailer bass.

The contrast is the point: Niko fidgets; Zero floats; the Tailor waits.

Visual correction: use CAST.md and zilkroad-originals/ for identity. The earlier generated cast board and costume sheets were rejected for drifting from the original NFTs.

## Visual and audio grammar

Ledger City: wet blue-green streets, warm amber restaurant windows, cubic silhouettes. Start with actual room tone, a refrigerator hum and a vibrating phone. Music enters only when the Tailors notice the first payment. Leave both YOU DIED cards almost silent. Third life brings daylight and kitchen sounds, not a victory explosion.

Human performances on voxel bodies: breathe, swallow, interrupt, hesitate, laugh through fear. Use body tilt, controlled pauses, movement and head angle, preserving the original square eye designs. Zero has no mouth: communicate through eye scale, arm movements and head tilt; his voice is spatial but clear. Keep all NFT faces intact, with no invented mouths, noses or hair. Body extensions are not yet locked; original NFT busts are authoritative.

Phone screens, transaction amounts, labels and subtitles are separate editorial overlays. Generate clean screen plates rather than expecting a video model to preserve small text. Every amount is illustrative game currency. Show **PRACTICE WALLET · SIMULATED ZEC** at first wallet reveal and on the end card. Rewinds reset game state, not blockchain transactions.

## Script

### 00:00–00:14 — Wake up

Black. Three phone vibrations. Rain against a small apartment window. Niko reaches out from under a blanket, misses the phone, opens one eye. His voice is unperformed, half inside a yawn.

**ROOK — phone; warm, already working:** “Morning, kid. You earned five ZEC. Set up your wallet. I'll send it over.”

**NIKO — sleepy, sitting up on “five”:** “Five? …Morning's improving.”

Hold on the tiny involuntary smile. He checks whether the call is still connected before sitting straighter.

### 00:14–00:29 — The first wallet

Over Niko's shoulder. Three readable beats: CREATE WALLET → BACK UP SECURELY → RECEIVE. Recovery details never appear on screen. Niko sends his public receiving address to Rook. Close on a confirmation, then the balance changes.

**ROOK — practical, softer on the warning:** “Back it up somewhere safe. Keep the recovery phrase to yourself.”

**NIKO — alert now:** “Ready.”

**ROOK:** “Sent.”

**NIKO — small disbelieving laugh:** “It's here.”

Overlay: **RECEIVED · 5 ZEC · TRANSPARENT**. Label the public address as PUBLIC. This establishes receiving earned ZEC, not mining coins by tapping a button.

### 00:29–00:44 — Breakfast money

Door opens into Ledger City. A short walk: noodle steam, a courier squeezing past, the shop bell. Auntie Node is wiping the same chipped dark ceramic bowl we will see in every life.

**AUNTIE NODE — fond, brisk, teasing:** “You look employed.”

**NIKO — grinning, too proud:** “Temporarily. Extra noodles.”

He pays to the restaurant's transparent address. Cut to the Tailor's screen: public address, amount, time. A separate café camera thumbnail links that payment to Niko's face. The blockchain does not supply a street location or a real name.

**NEEDLE — close radio whisper:** “Payment matches. Café camera. Brown face.”

### 00:44–01:00 — The invitation

Niko senses the room change. Auntie stops wiping. Hem fills the doorway; Needle steps out of the rain behind him. No guns needed. The Tailor tilts his olive cap and walks into the light.

**TAILOR — courteous, unhurried; slight smile only on “quiet”:** “Five ZEC. Congratulations. Most people celebrate somewhere… quiet.”

**NIKO — tries a smile; it fails:** “Do I know you?”

**TAILOR — a full beat, then almost intimate:** “You made that unnecessary.”

Niko backs into a chair. Ceramic scrapes. Hem's shadow closes over the lens. Cut before contact.

### 01:00–01:07 — First death

Absolute black. **YOU DIED.**

One breath, cut short. No gore, no impact montage. Hold the card long enough to sting. A white cube drifts into the black.

### 01:07–01:28 — Zero

Niko is on his back in an endless dark space. Zero hangs upside down above him, studying him. Small, white, impossible. A head tilt replaces a smirk.

**ZERO — dry curiosity; let the pause do the joke:** “Well. That was brief.”

**NIKO — breathless, touches his chest:** “Am I dead?”

**ZERO — gently matter-of-fact:** “Yes. You're taking it quite well.”

**NIKO — looks around; anger covering fear:** “Who are you?”

**ZERO — finally level with his eyes:** “Zero. You left a public trail. They followed it.”

A white cube becomes a rewind icon. Zero offers it, waits for Niko to reach.

**ZERO — warmth beneath the mischief:** “Again. This time, shield the funds.”

### 01:28–01:47 — Second life: the shield

Same phone vibration. Same hand reaching. This time Niko catches it immediately. Use matched camera positions; the repetition must feel intentional. The practice payment resets with the scene.

**NIKO — low, focused:** “Okay. Wallet. Payment. Shield.”

He chooses SHIELD. Hold on **TRANSPARENT → SHIELDED** and a confirmed state. A filmic gold trace becomes diffuse inside the shielded pool; the previous public entry remains faintly visible behind it. This is an explanatory overlay, not literal camouflage.

**ZERO — patient, precise:** “Shielding protects what comes next. It doesn't erase what was already public.”

Niko looks up from the phone. The street is still full of people who can physically see him.

### 01:47–02:08 — The wrong shortcut

Exchange window. Niko rushes through a notice: TRANSPARENT RECIPIENT. He sends nearly the whole balance back out shortly after shielding. Hold on **UNSHIELD · PUBLIC RECIPIENT + AMOUNT** before confirmation.

**NIKO — relieved too soon:** “There. Safe.”

The Tailor's display shows two public boundary amounts, nearby times, and a dotted connection labeled POSSIBLE MATCH. Needle checks a street camera and points. The dotted line becomes a confirmed physical sighting, not a cryptographic proof of the shielded transfer.

**TAILOR — quietly pleased:** “Almost the same amount. Minutes apart. Check the camera.”

Niko sees Hem's reflection in the glass. His hand freezes above the receipt.

**NIKO — tiny, involuntary:** “Oh, come on.”

Hard black. **YOU DIED. AGAIN.** Hold two seconds.

### 02:08–02:27 — The actual lesson

Niko sits beside Zero in the void. No grand staging now; two exhausted people on an invisible curb.

**NIKO — frustration breaks into a genuine question:** “I shielded it!”

**ZERO — no joke this time:** “Then you exposed the recipient and amount. They guessed. The camera did the rest.”

**NIKO — quieter:** “So what do I do?”

**ZERO — kind, direct:** “Use shielded payments when you can. Know what you're revealing when you can't.”

Niko reaches for the rewind cube without being prompted.

### 02:27–02:45 — Third life: send and receive

Phone vibration. Niko is already dressed. Three measured inserts: recipient supports shielded receipt; review recipient and amount; confirm. Mika waits at an arcade counter, taps her phone when Niko's shielded test payment arrives.

**MIKA — bright, casual:** “Got it. Sending yours back.”

Cut to Niko: **SHIELDED PAYMENT RECEIVED**. His shoulders drop. Keep confirmation timing an editorial time cut, not an instant-settlement promise.

**NIKO — the first honest laugh:** “Oh. That's it?”

**ZERO — a trace of a smile in the voice:** “That's it.”

On-screen chapter stamps, timed to the actions: **SET UP · GET ZEC · SHIELD · SEND · RECEIVE**. Brief recap insert: **UNSHIELD = PUBLIC OUTPUT**, from the prior life. No extra exposition voiceover.

### 02:45–03:03 — The same bowl

Same café framing. Same dark ceramic bowl. Niko checks the payment route; Auntie presents a shielded payment request. He reviews it, pays, and waits for confirmation. Her phone confirms. He takes his first bite.

**AUNTIE NODE — recognizing his expression, not the time loop:** “Extra noodles?”

**NIKO — soft, present, finally hungry:** “Please.”

Cut to the Tailor. His screen has no public shielded payment details to connect to the café. No magical deletion of his old evidence. He turns the phone face down.

**TAILOR — controlled irritation, barely audible:** “I dislike surprises.”

Return to Niko. He notices the street, then chooses to finish breakfast. This is a lesson completed, not a claim that surveillance has ceased to exist.

### 03:03–03:20 — Title and invitation

Niko leaves the café into dawn. Zero floats in the window reflection for a moment, then is gone.

**ZERO — grounded, warm; let “yours” settle:** “Your money. Your choices. Your life.”

Title: **LOSE THE TAIL**

Subline: **Learn Zcash. One life at a time.**

End card: **PLAY THE PRACTICE GAME**
**lose-the-tail.vercel.app**
**Simulated funds. Real privacy lessons.**

Final sound: the café bell. One clean ring. Hold the readable end card for five seconds minimum.

## Editorial notes

- 3:20 is a first pacing target, not an audio-measured runtime. Record a scratch read before locking cuts; preserve space for reactions rather than accelerating every line.
- This is a story trailer for a learning game. Label cinematic reenactments if they are presented alongside actual gameplay, and use actual interface capture for any promised interaction demonstration.
- The death loop dramatizes a privacy lesson. Public transactions do not automatically identify a person or cause physical harm; the gang uses fictional off-chain cameras and prior observations.
- Shielding does not erase prior public history. Shielded-to-shielded payments keep their payment details out of public blockchain view; participating parties still know their own transaction information.
- Unshielding is a legitimate operation. Its transparent output reveals public information; similar amounts and timing can support inference, not guaranteed linkage. Do not imply a timer, split amount, or single button guarantees anonymity.
- No swap lesson in this cut: the requested wallet setup, getting ZEC, shielding/unshielding, sending and receiving already form the complete dramatic arc. Earned payment is the getting-ZEC route shown here; it is not a claim that wallets dispense free ZEC.
